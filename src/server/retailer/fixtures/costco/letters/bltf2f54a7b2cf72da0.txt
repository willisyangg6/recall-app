Dear Costco Member,
Costco records indicate that you, or one of your add-on members, have purchased a Goal Zero Yeti 3000X
bundle (Items #1507511, #2507511, #1614576, and #1644157) between November 2020 and August
2026.
Goal Zero, in cooperation with the Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC), is voluntarily recalling
the Yeti 3000X Portable Power Station. The Power Stations may overheat, posing a risk of serious burn
injury and fire hazard. No injuries have been reported as a result of this fault.
Consumers should immediately stop using the power stations and install the YETI 3000X firmware update
in the app or contact Goal Zero if support is needed to install a free repair in the form of a firmware update.
The firmware update is the recall remedy and does not require returning the product to the original place of
purchase - whether this be online or a retail location.
If you are not currently using the Goal Zero app, download it for free from the Apple or Google Play store.
Install the firmware update by tapping the banner in your app or by going to Settings>Firmware Update.
Reach out to the Goal Zero Customer Support team by phone at 1-888-794-6250. Representatives are
available Monday through Friday from 9:00 AM to 2:00 PM Mountain Time (MST). You may also submit a
support ticket through our Help Center. For more information and detailed firmware update steps, visit
GoalZero.com.
Thank you,
