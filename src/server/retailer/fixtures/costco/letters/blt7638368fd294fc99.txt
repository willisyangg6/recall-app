April 7, 2026
Dear Costco Member,
Our records indicate that you or one of your add on members may have purchased item # 2000012
Traditional Madeleines, which was inadvertently packaged with Madeleines filled with Chocolate
Hazelnut. The mislabeled units contain undeclared Tree Nuts (Hazelnut/Filbert) and soy.
If you are allergic to Hazelnuts/Filberts or soy, do not consume
this product. Please return it to Costco for a full refund.
People with a hazelnut, filbert or soy allergy or severe
sensitivity risk a life-threatening allergic reaction if they
consume this product.
Affected products were distributed between 3/30/2026 and 4/6/2026 at the following Costco
warehouses:
Missoula, MT Burlington, WA East Peoria, IL
Bellevue, WI Pleasant Prairie, WI Chico, CA
Thornton, CO Gilroy, CA Bayamon, PR
Pembroke Pines, FL Fairfax, VA
You can visit https://customerservice.costco.com with your questions.
We apologize for any inconvenience this recall may have caused.
Sincerely,
Costco Wholesale
