Important Product Safety Recall Notification
Dear Costco Member,
Costco records indicate that you, or one of your add-on members, have purchased one or more of the
items listed below between April 1st, 2025 and June 17, 2026.
You are receiving this email because the product you purchased may be included in a product safety
recall. This recall pertains to Mistolin and Scented Lestoil products with the UPC codes displaying
a lot code beginning with “PR01” and followed by a five-digit number between 25091 and 26168.
The recalled products may contain bacteria, including Pseudomonas aeruginosa, which is an
environmental organism found widely in soil and water. People with weakened immune systems or
external medical devices who are exposed to Pseudomonas aeruginosa face a risk of serious infection
that may require medical treatment. The bacteria can enter the body if inhaled, through the eyes, or
through a break in the skin. People with healthy immune systems are usually not affected by bacteria.
Please immediately stop using Mistolin and Scented Lestoil products included in the recall. You can
request a refund by visiting mistolinrecall.com, or by returning the item to your local Costco for a full
refund. Please call the Mistolin/Lestoil Recall Center at 855-215-5439 if you have any issues or concerns.
To bring this matter to the attention of consumers, Clorox is also posting communications at
https://puerto-rico.mistolin.co/.
