IMPORTANT SAFETY RECALL
Dear Valued Customer:
Sauna360 alongside the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC) is recalling benches in
certain Tylö Halmstad and Kiruna Hybrid Saunas to address a safety concern. Sauna360 is providing a
remedy for all Tylö Halmstad and Kiruna Hybrid Saunas sold since product launch in September 2024.
The CPSC has approved an enhanced remedy consisting of additional hardware and bench
supports. The enhanced remedy will be installed on all affected products in the field at no charge to
you. The installation of the remedy should take about an hour. Until the recall remedy is installed,
you should not use your sauna.
