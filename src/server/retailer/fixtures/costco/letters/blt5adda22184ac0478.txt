Dear Costco Member,
Costco records indicate that you, or one of your add-on members, have purchased item
#1081557 Ajinomoto Shoyu Ramen Bowls between 12/01/2024 – 3/12/2026.
Ajinomoto Foods North America in cooperation with the USDA announced a voluntary recall
of select lots of Shoyu Ramen Bowls due to the potential presence of glass in the product.
The affected products have expanded and are coded with Best By dates ranging from
20 MAY 2026 through 06 JUL 2027.
The Best By date can be found printed on the side of the box or on individual packages in
the format DD MMM YYYY (Day, Month, Year).
There have been no confirmed reports of injuries related to the recalled product.
Please check if you have this item and you may return it to your local Costco for a full
refund.
