May 30, 2026
Dear Costco Member,
Costco records indicate that you, or one of your add-on members, have purchased Motor City Pizza Co. 5
Cheese Bread (Item# 1453434) between 2/6/2026 and 5/29/2026.
Out of an abundance of caution, Champion Foods LLC, is voluntarily recalling certain batches of Motor
City Pizza Co. 5 Cheese Bread because they have the potential to be contaminated with Salmonella. This
action follows a California Dairies, Inc. milk powder recall due to a concern of potential Salmonella
contamination. The recalled milk powder was supplied to a third-party manufacturer that provides a
seasoning blend used in our 5 cheese sauce blend.
The following SELL BY dates are affected:
2/3/2027, 2/4/2027, 2/24/2027, 2/25/2027, 3/10/2027, 3/11/2027, 3/18/2027, 3/25/2027
You can find this code in the following format printed in black ink in the image on the front of the
box as shown in the red circle on the packaging.
There have been no reports of illness or injury related to these products. Routine testing conducted by the
seasonings blend manufacturer prior to use in the production of the 5 Cheese Bread showed that the
seasonings batches tested negative for Salmonella.
Please return the item to your local Costco for a full refund. Please do not consume, serve, use, sell, or
distribute the recalled product.
Consumers who purchased one of the recalled lots listed above may contact Champion Foods LLC
directly info@championfoods.com for more information.
