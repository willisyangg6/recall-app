IMPORTANT PRODUCT SAFETY RECALL NOTICE
Dear Costco Member,
Costco records indicate that you, or one of your add-on members, purchased a Titan
Pro 40 Can Soft Sided Iceless Collapsible Cooler, Costco Item Number 1872184
between March 6, 2026 and March 29, 2026.
In conjunction with the US Consumer Product Safety Commission, California
Innovations is recalling the cooler. It is possible that one small, powerful magnet could
detach/fall out from the cooler’s latch if the cover over the magnet was inadequately
sealed in the manufacturing process. Small powerful magnets, which are not securely
contained within a product, can pose a choking hazard for children and pets.
PLEASE STOP USING THE COOLER and contact California Innovations to receive a
free replacement latch kit.
Call California Innovations Inc. at 1-888-413-2665 between 9:00 AM and 5:00 PM
Eastern Time if you have any issues or concerns. Recall information can be found on
our website www.arcticzone.com.
