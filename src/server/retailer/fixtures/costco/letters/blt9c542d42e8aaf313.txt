November 7, 2025
Dear Costco Member,
Costco and Ventura foods are announcing the recall of select Sell By dates of Item# 19927
Caesar Salad and Item# 11444 Chicken Sandwich w/ Caesar Salad due to the possible presence
of plastic foreign material in the salad dressing of the items.
Affected products are labeled with Sell By Dates between 10/17/25 and 11/9/25.
Please stop eating the product and return the item to your local Costco for a full refund.
You can go to https://customerservice.costco.com/ with your questions.
Thank you
