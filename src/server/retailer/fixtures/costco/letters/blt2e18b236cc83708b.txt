Important Product Information Notice
Desert Willow Plants
Item #25215
Dear Costco Member,
We are contacting you because Costco records show that you, or one of your add-on members, purchased desert
willow (item #25215) between June 24 – July 3, 2026.
It has come to our attention that these plants may be infested with the Glassy-Winged Sharpshooter (GWSS). This
invasive insect pest can spread harmful plant diseases, including Pierce’s disease, which can kill grapevines. The pest
can also damage citrus trees, landscape plants and other crops. Early detection and rapid response are critical to
preventing the pest from spreading further.
What To Do:
1. Isolate the Plant
• Keep the plant in its original pot or container and away from other plants.
• Do not put the plant in the ground if you have not already done so.
• If possible, place the plant inside two trash bags, one inside the other, and seal them closed.
• Do not return, transport or relocate the plant.
• Do not place the plant in the trash or compost bin.
2. Contact Your Local County Agricultural Commissioner’s Office Right Away at
https://www.cdfa.ca.gov/exec/county/countymap/
• An agricultural inspector may come to look at the plant and nearby host plants.
• If inspectors find GWSS, they will safely remove and dispose of the plant.
• GWSS monitoring traps may also be placed on or near the property.
DO NOT RETURN THE PLANT TO COSTCO, instead please present this letter to your local warehouse to receive a full
refund. Learn more at: cdfa.ca.gov/pdcp/gwss-costco-plants.html
We apologize for any inconvenience this has caused and appreciate your attention and cooperation in this matter. Your
support helps safeguard California’s agriculture.
