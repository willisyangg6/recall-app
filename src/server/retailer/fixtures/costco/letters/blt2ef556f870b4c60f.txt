Dear Costco Member,
Costco records indicate that you, or one of your add-on members, have purchased item # 1652818 Generac GP9200E
Gas Generator between 5/1/2025 – 2/28/2026.
When first filling the recalled generator with gasoline, fuel can leak from the carburetor posing a serious risk of injury or
death from fire or burn hazard. Members with generators that have previously been filled with enough gasoline to move
the gauge off "E" or have been used without any gasoline leakage, can continue use.
The affected serial number range is: 3016786070 – 3016788388.
Members should immediately stop using the recalled Generac portable generator and go
to https://www.generac.com/about/recalls/GP-Carburetor to confirm if your serial number is included in this recall. If your
item is impacted by this recall and has either not yet been filled with fuel or exhibited leakage after fueling as described
above, please contact your dealer to arrange for a free repair at the dealer’s location. Alternatively, members may return
the item to your local Costco for a full refund.
For more information please call Generac at 800-396-9951 from 8:00 A.M. to 5:00 P.M. CT Monday through Friday or
online at https://www.generac.com/about/recalls/GP-Carburetor or www.generac.com and click on “Important Recall
Information”.
Thank you,
Generac Power Systems
