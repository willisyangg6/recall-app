# Lotly Consumer Health Data Policy

**Effective date:** ⟦FOUNDER: set at publication⟧

This policy is about one kind of information: the **food allergens** you
choose to watch in Lotly. Some laws, including Washington's and Nevada's,
treat information about allergies as "consumer health data". Our full
Privacy Policy covers everything else: ⟦FOUNDER: Privacy Policy URL⟧.

Lotly is operated by Willis Yang. Contact: **lotlysupport@gmail.com**.

## What we collect, and how we use it

- **What:** the food allergens you select to watch. There are nine:
  peanuts, tree nuts, milk, egg, wheat, soy, sesame, fish and crustacean
  shellfish. They may be your own, or those of anyone you shop or cook for.
  We do not collect symptoms, diagnoses, or any other health information.
- **Where it comes from:** only you, when you select allergens in the app.
- **How it is used:** on your iPhone, your allergens show which recalls may
  matter to you. **Only if you consent**, they are also stored on Lotly's
  server and used for one purpose: deciding which recall alerts to send you.
- **What we never do:** use them for advertising, sell them, or use them to
  track you across other apps or websites.
- **If you do not consent:** your allergens stay only on your iPhone, and
  Lotly still uses them there. Recall alerts are then not based on
  allergens. ⟦PLANNED: consent sheet, proposal §1⟧

## Who receives it

We share consumer health data only with the service providers that process
it for us, under our instructions, to provide the service you asked for:

- **Supabase** (database hosting, Northern California, USA) stores your
  allergens with a random installation identifier.
- **Expo** and **Apple** deliver recall alerts. The alert text contains
  only public recall facts. Because an alert is sent to you because of your
  choices, its delivery could indicate an allergen you watch.

We do not sell consumer health data, and we do not share it with any other
third party or affiliate. No third party collects it over time and across
different websites or apps through Lotly.

⟦UNCERTAIN: Nevada's definition of "share" has no exception for service
providers. Your consent covers these providers, but whether that is enough
is untested.⟧

## Your rights and choices

- **Withdraw consent at any time.** Use Profile → Privacy & Data Controls →
  "Use my allergens for alerts", or clear your allergens. The copy on our
  server is removed as soon as your iPhone is online; if it is offline, the
  app shows that removal is pending. ⟦PLANNED: proposal §1.3⟧
- **Delete.** "Reset app and delete my data" deletes everything our server
  holds for this installation, immediately. A deleted record can remain in
  a database backup for up to 7 days. If you stop using Lotly, our server
  deletes your data automatically after 12 months.
- **Access.** "Show what Lotly's server holds", in the app, shows the data
  stored for your installation and the list of service providers above.
  ⟦PLANNED: proposal §3.1⟧
- **Requests by email.** In the app, tap "Get a request code" (Privacy &
  Data Controls), choose a copy of your data or deletion, then email the
  code to lotlysupport@gmail.com. ⟦PLANNED: proposal §3.2⟧
  - **Why a code.** Lotly has no accounts, so the code is how we confirm the
    request comes from your installation.
  - **What the code can do.** It works once, expires after 14 days, and
    only lets us find your installation for the request you chose. We never
    ask for your installation identifier, and you should never send it.
  - **If you no longer have the app.** Reinstall Lotly on the same iPhone.
    Your installation usually comes back from the phone's secure storage,
    and you can then use the in-app options or get a code.
  - **If that isn't possible** (a new or erased phone), we cannot confirm
    which data is yours. We will reply in writing, explain why, and tell you
    how to appeal.
- **Deadlines.**
  - We respond within 45 days. We may extend that once by 45 days, and we
    will tell you if we do.
  - Deletion requests confirmed with a code are completed within 30 days;
    in practice, immediately, with backups expiring within 7 days.
- **Appeal.** If we decline your request, reply to our answer with the word
  "Appeal". We will answer in writing within 45 days. If we deny your
  appeal, we will tell you how to contact your state attorney general.
- **No charge.** We do not charge for requests, and we never treat you
  differently for using these rights.

## Changes to this policy

If we want to collect a new kind of consumer health data, or use it for a
new purpose, we will ask for your consent first. For other material
changes, we will give notice in the app before they take effect, and update
the effective date above.
